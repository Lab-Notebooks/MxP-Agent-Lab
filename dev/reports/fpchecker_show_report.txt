========================================
         FPChecker Configuration        
========================================

Installation path: /usr/local

Add the following to CFLAGS and/or CXXFLAGS:

(1) For exceptions checking:
-g -include /usr/local/src/Runtime_cpu.h -fpass-plugin=/usr/local/lib/libfpchecker_cpu.so

(2) For rounding error tracking:
-g -fno-vectorize -fno-slp-vectorize -include /usr/local/src/Runtime_error.h -fpass-plugin=/usr/local/lib/libfpchecker_error.so

Wrappers are located here:
/usr/local/bin/clang-fpchecker
/usr/local/bin/clang++-fpchecker
/usr/local/bin/mpicc-fpchecker
/usr/local/bin/mpicxx-fpchecker
